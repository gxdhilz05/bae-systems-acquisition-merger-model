# Stage 1: Target-Screening Methodology

## Purpose

The screen is designed to rank Chemring, Cohort and Avon Technologies consistently before a preferred target is selected for detailed valuation and merger modelling.

## Proposed scorecard

| Category | Weight | Core question |
|---|---:|---|
| Strategic and product fit | 25% | Does the target strengthen capabilities BAE wants to own? |
| Growth and financial quality | 20% | Is the business growing profitably with credible earnings and cash generation? |
| Valuation and affordability | 20% | Can BAE offer a sufficient premium without overpaying? |
| Synergy potential | 15% | Can ownership by BAE create identifiable, achievable benefits? |
| Financing and leverage impact | 10% | Can BAE fund the deal while maintaining a sensible capital structure? |
| Execution and regulatory risk | 10% | What could prevent completion or reduce the value captured? |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

Each company will receive a score from 1 to 5 in every category. The weighted score is:

```text
Weighted score = category score / 5 x category weight
```

The numerical result will organise the judgment rather than replace it. Every score must be supported by a concise explanation and a traceable source.

## Information required

### Strategic and product fit

- Products and technical capabilities
- End markets and programmes
- Customers and geographic exposure
- Relationship with BAE's existing portfolio
- Capability gaps that the acquisition could fill

### Growth and financial quality

- Historical and expected revenue growth
- Operating or EBITDA margins
- Order intake and backlog where relevant
- Cash conversion
- Customer and programme concentration

### Valuation and affordability

- Equity value and enterprise value
- Current trading multiples
- Likely takeover premium
- Implied transaction size relative to BAE

### Synergy potential

- Duplicated corporate costs
- Procurement or manufacturing opportunities
- Cross-selling and programme access
- Technology integration opportunities
- Timing, cost and risk of delivery

### Financing and leverage impact

- BAE cash capacity
- Incremental debt capacity
- Potential share issuance
- Pro forma leverage and interest coverage

### Execution and regulatory risk

- Competition and national-security review
- Customer approval or change-of-control exposure
- Integration complexity
- Cultural and operational risks
- Risk that the required premium absorbs the expected synergies

## Guardrails

- Do not choose a winner before completing the evidence-backed screen.
- Do not award high synergy scores without identifying the mechanism.
- Do not confuse a high-quality company with an attractive acquisition at any price.
- Keep assumptions separate from reported company information.
- Record the publication date and URL for every external source.
