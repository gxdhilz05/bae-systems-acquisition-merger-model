# Stage 2C: Simple CCA and PTA

## Conclusion

The market-based valuation cross-checks support a higher value than the standalone DCF, but they do not justify paying away all of the acquisition synergies.

| Method | Implied value per Cohort share |
|---|---:|
| Standalone DCF | 1,076p |
| Comparable-company analysis | 1,489p |
| Precedent-transactions analysis | 1,337p |
| Synergy-supported maximum price | 1,293p |

The recommendation remains to pursue Cohort only below approximately 1,300p per share.

## Comparable-company analysis

CCA asks: **How does the stock market value similar listed companies today?**

The peer set is deliberately small and easy to defend:

| Company | Why it is comparable | EV / adjusted operating profit |
|---|---|---:|
| QinetiQ | UK defence technology and services | 12.3x |
| Chemring | UK defence products and technology | 21.6x |
| Avon Technologies | UK listed specialist defence supplier | 18.8x |
| Median | Valuation reference | 18.8x |

The calculation is:

> Implied Cohort enterprise value = 18.8x × £36.3m adjusted operating profit

This gives an implied enterprise value of approximately £683.0m. Adding £2.2m of net funds gives approximately £685.2m of equity value, or 1,489p per share.

## Precedent-transactions analysis

PTA asks: **What multiples did buyers pay for previous defence businesses?**

| Transaction | Announced EV / EBITDA |
|---|---:|
| QinetiQ / Avantus Federal | 14.6x |
| BAE Systems / Ball Aerospace | 13.0x |
| Honeywell / CAES | 14.0x |
| Median | 14.0x |

Cohort adjusted EBITDA is approximated as adjusted operating profit plus depreciation of owned and right-of-use assets:

> £36.3m + £4.6m + £2.9m = £43.8m

Applying the 14.0x median gives an enterprise value of approximately £613.3m. Adding net funds gives approximately £615.5m of equity value, or 1,337p per share.

## Why the methods disagree

CCA reflects current stock-market valuations. PTA includes the price buyers paid for control and may also include expected synergies. The DCF is based on Cohort's own forecast cash flows. Different methods therefore produce different answers.

The range is useful, but it does not mean BAE should automatically pay the highest value. BAE should retain part of the value created by the combination.

## Interview explanation

> "I used CCA to compare Cohort with listed UK defence companies and PTA to compare it with previous defence acquisitions. The peer median implied about 1,489p per share, while the transaction median implied about 1,337p. I treated both as valuation cross-checks rather than exact answers because the companies and transactions are not perfectly comparable. Together with the DCF and synergy analysis, this supported a disciplined price below approximately 1,300p."

## Limitations

- The peer set is small because few listed UK defence specialists closely resemble Cohort.
- Market values are point-in-time estimates and will change with share prices.
- The precedent transactions are larger and more US-focused than Cohort.
- The announced transaction multiples use different tax and synergy adjustments.
- EBITDA does not capture capital expenditure or working-capital requirements.
